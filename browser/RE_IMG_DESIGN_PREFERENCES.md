# re-img design preference

Emphasize minimal vertical space. Prefer compact horizontal controls and brief contextual help over stacked label/value blocks or expanded panels. Keep text legible, keyboard/touch interactions accessible and narrow layouts bounded. This preference guides scoped re-img changes; it does not authorize unrelated redesign.

Metrics: total downloaded images to the left of estimated time saved, in one horizontal row. On narrow screens use concise EN/FR visual labels while retaining full accessible labels and exact values. Extreme values may visually ellipsize rather than overflow; full text remains accessible and available as a value title. Calculation/count semantics are unchanged.

Latest sidebar direction: keep filename controls expanded; save space with five visible scrollable preset rows. Keep the Presets title, omit its visible count and separate shortcut icon, and place brief shortcut guidance first in the existing top-right question-mark help content.
